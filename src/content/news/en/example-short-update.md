This is the simplest kind of post: a short update. Use it for announcements such as a new team member, a talk you are giving, or a tool release.

Keep it to a few paragraphs. You can still add [links like this one](https://barghest.asia/research/) and **bold text** where it helps.

That's it. The title, date and one-line description at the top of the file are all the list page needs.
