BARGHEST is a non-profit research and development group working with civil society. We investigate software exploitation and build open-source tools and methods for mobile security research and digital forensics.

Our work supports researchers, journalists, and communities facing spyware, malware, censorship, and digital repression. We publish our tools and methods so others can inspect, reproduce, and adapt them.
